Before You Apply

Eligibility and Documents

Eligibility requirements vary by lender. They may assess the following factors:

  • Age and residency criteria
  • Income and employment details
  • Credit history and existing repayments
  • Identity and address proof
  • Income proof and bank statements

Always confirm the required documents and eligibility criteria with the lender.

Frequently Asked Questions

Personal Loan FAQs

What is a personal loan?

A personal loan is money borrowed from a lender and repaid according to agreed terms, usually through scheduled instalments.

How can I estimate my monthly EMI?

Open the FinTrust EMI calculator and enter the principal amount, annual interest rate and repayment tenure to get an illustrative estimate.

Will a personal loan application be approved?

Approval depends on the lender's assessment and eligibility requirements. FinTrust does not guarantee approval.

Can I apply through this demo website?

This is a frontend demonstration. The application link is for showcasing the interface and does not submit a real loan application.
