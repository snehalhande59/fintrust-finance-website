Frequently Asked Questions

EMI Calculator FAQs

What is EMI?

EMI means Equated Monthly Instalment. It is the scheduled monthly payment towards a loan.

How is EMI calculated?

For a standard reducing-balance loan, EMI is calculated from the principal, periodic interest rate and number of monthly instalments.

Does a longer tenure reduce EMI?

A longer tenure generally reduces the monthly EMI for the same principal and rate, but can increase the total interest paid.

Is this the final EMI offered by a bank?

No. This is a demonstration calculator. Confirm the actual interest rate, fees and repayment schedule with your lender.
