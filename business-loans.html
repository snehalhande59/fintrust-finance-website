Prepare Before Applying

Business Loan Documents

Depending on the business structure and lender, you may be asked to provide the following documents.

  • Business registration and identity documents
  • PAN and applicable KYC documents
  • Business bank statements
  • Financial statements and income tax returns
  • Business plan or purpose of financing

Requirements vary. Confirm the exact checklist with your lender.

Frequently Asked Questions

Business Loan FAQs

What is a business loan?

A business loan is financing offered by a lender for eligible business purposes and repaid according to the agreed terms.

Can a small business explore financing?

Potentially, yes. Eligibility depends on the lender's criteria, business records, financial position and other applicable conditions.

Which documents are generally required?

Business registration, KYC, bank statements and financial records may be requested. Requirements vary by lender and product.

Does FinTrust guarantee business loan approval?

No. This is a frontend demo website. A lender must assess eligibility and confirm any actual loan offer.
