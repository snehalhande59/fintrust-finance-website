Documents You May Need

Requirements differ by lender, employment type, property and applicant profile. Commonly requested documents may include:

  • Identity and address proof
  • Salary slips or income proof
  • Recent bank statements
  • Income tax returns, where applicable
  • Property-related documents

Confirm the final checklist directly with your chosen lender.

Frequently Asked Questions

Home Loan FAQs

What is a home loan?

A home loan is financing provided by a lender for an eligible housing purpose. The borrower repays it according to the agreed terms.

How can I estimate my home loan EMI?

Use the FinTrust EMI calculator and enter the loan amount, annual interest rate and tenure. The result is an estimate, not a lender offer.

Which documents are needed for a home loan?

Identity, address, income, bank and property documents are commonly requested. The exact list depends on the lender and application.

Does FinTrust guarantee loan approval?

No. This is a frontend demonstration website. Eligibility, interest rates and approvals must be confirmed by an authorised lender.
